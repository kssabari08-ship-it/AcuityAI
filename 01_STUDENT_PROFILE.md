## 5. Cognitive Ergonomics & Learner Constraints

1. **Chronobiology / Energy Curve**:
   * *Morning (Weekend 09:00 - 11:30)*: High focus. Reserve for heavy mathematical derivations, algorithmic logic, and physics calculus.
   * *Weekday Evenings (17:30 - 20:30)*: Moderate focus. Reserve for active recall problem sets and organic reaction pathways.
   * *Late Night (After 21:00)*: Cognitive fatigue. Strict blackout on heavy analytical work; light active flashcards or rest only.
2. **Break Frequency Rules**:
   * Strictly 50 minutes study + 10 minutes non-screen break (Pomodoro 50/10).
   * Minimum 30-minute buffer before bed to preserve melatonin secretion.
3. **Pedagogical Revision Modalities**:
   * *Active Retrieval Practice* (Past paper questions, timed exercises).
   * *Feynman Technique* (Explaining concepts simply without notes).
   * *Interleaving* (Switching subjects daily rather than doing 3 days of one subject).
   * *Spaced Repetition* (Revisiting weak topics at Days 1, 3, 7, and 14).
