### Required AI Skills Satisfied (6 of Topics 1–9)
1. **AI Foundations**: Understanding LLM context windows, stochastic generation, deterministic vs. probabilistic outputs, and cognitive framing.
2. **Prompt Engineering**: System persona design, few-shot prompting, negative constraints, chain-of-thought planning, and strict tabular schemas.
3. **Automation & Workflows**: Generation of programmatic calendar standards (`.ics` RFC 5545, Google Calendar CSV) to eliminate manual event entry.
4. **Productivity & Workspace Architecture**: Two-way linked Notion databases, Kanban sprint boards, and Pomodoro time-blocking systems.
5. **Responsible AI & Ergonomics**: Bias against burnout, algorithmic pacing, cognitive fatigue management, boundary enforcement, and data privacy.
6. **Multimedia, Audio & Presentation Design**: Scripting, slide layout architecture, multimodal explainer design, and interactive web visualization.
